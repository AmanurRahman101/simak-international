About Genesis

A newer approach to health technology.

Since 2015, Genesis Trading Co. has imported and distributed clinical equipment for Bangladesh’s hospitals and laboratories — pairing global brands with local engineering.

Built for hospitals that need more than a shipment.

Genesis is an importer first: we source diagnostics, imaging, dialysis, and life-support systems from established international manufacturers, then deliver them through a Dhaka-based organization with nationwide reach.

Our team of 110+ professionals — including 60+ field engineers — handles installation, commissioning, user training, and after-sales service. That combination is why institutional customers and tender boards trust us with complex clinical projects.

Among our institutional relationships, Bangladesh Police Hospital stands out as a flagship government healthcare partner — a testament to our ability to support high-visibility, high-responsibility clinical environments.
